Land, Acreage and Estate Homes in Palm Beach County

Where to find acreage and estate homes in Palm Beach County: Loxahatchee, The Acreage, Manalapan, Hypoluxo and Boca Raton.

Some buyers want room: acreage for horses, space for a workshop, or an estate with water on both sides. Palm Beach County offers both ends of that spectrum, from rural lots in the west to some of the most exclusive waterfront addresses on the east coast.

Land and space out west

Loxahatchee and Loxahatchee Groves offer multi-acre properties with barns, paddocks and few neighbors. Browse Loxahatchee homes and acreage for sale.

The Acreage has 1.25-acre and larger lots with room for pools, RVs and outbuildings. See homes for sale in The Acreage.

Waterfront estates on the coast

Manalapan stretches between the ocean and the Intracoastal, with some of the largest estates in South Florida. View Manalapan estates for sale.

Hypoluxo is a small, quiet town with Intracoastal homes and private docks. Check waterfront homes in Hypoluxo.

Boca Raton offers luxury on many levels, from Royal Palm Yacht Club estates to gated golf communities in the west. Explore Boca Raton luxury homes for sale.

What to check on large or luxury properties

  • Survey, easements and zoning for land uses
  • Seawall, dock and lift condition on waterfront homes
  • Flood zone, elevation and insurance costs
  • Permits for barns, guest houses and additions
