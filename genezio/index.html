Selling an Inherited or Probate Home in Palm Beach County

A plain guide to selling an inherited home through probate in Palm Beach County, Florida.

Inheriting a home in Palm Beach County often comes with a long list of questions. Who has the authority to sell? Does it have to go through probate? What is it worth? Here is a plain overview of how probate home sales usually work in Florida.

Who can sell an inherited home?

In most cases the court appoints a personal representative, sometimes called an executor, who has the authority to sell the property once the court allows it. If the home was in a trust, the trustee may be able to sell without probate. A Florida probate attorney can confirm which applies.

Find out what the home is worth

The value at the date of death matters for taxes and for dividing the estate between heirs. Request a free home value estimate to start, and your attorney may also order a formal appraisal.

Prepare the property

  • Secure the home, keep insurance active and maintain the yard and pool
  • Remove personal belongings once heirs agree on what to keep
  • Decide whether to sell as-is or make light repairs
  • Gather the deed, tax bills, HOA documents and utility accounts
