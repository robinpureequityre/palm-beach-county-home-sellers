Affordable Neighborhoods in South Florida for Value Buyers

Six South Florida areas where first-time and value buyers get more home for the money: Lake Worth Beach, Palm Springs, Lantana, Riviera Beach, Pompano Beach and Hollywood.

South Florida prices have risen fast, but there are still neighborhoods where first-time buyers and value-minded buyers can get closer to the coast without stretching too far. These six areas often offer more home for the money than their better-known neighbors.

Palm Beach County

Lake Worth Beach has older cottages and bungalows near a lively downtown and its own public beach. Browse Lake Worth Beach homes for sale.

Palm Springs is a small village with affordable single-family homes and condos in the heart of the county. See homes for sale in Palm Springs, FL.

Lantana offers coastal living at a lower price point than Boynton Beach or Delray Beach. View Lantana real estate.

Riviera Beach has mainland homes close to the marina and Singer Island at prices well below the island itself. Check Riviera Beach homes for sale.

Broward County

Pompano Beach is changing quickly with a redeveloped beachfront, while many neighborhoods remain affordable. See homes for sale in Pompano Beach.

Hollywood offers mid-century homes, a walkable downtown and beach access without Fort Lauderdale prices. Explore Hollywood homes for sale.

Making a lower budget go further

  • Get pre-approved first so you know your real budget
  • Factor in insurance and flood zones, not just the price
  • Look at homes that need cosmetic updates rather than major repairs
  • Ask about first-time buyer assistance programs
