Palm Beach County Home Seller Resource Center

Local selling guides from Pure Equity Realty US

Selling a home in Palm Beach County starts with the same few questions no matter where you live: what is it worth, what should I fix, how long will it take and who should list it? The answers change a lot from city to city, though. This resource center collects our local selling guides in one place so you can start with the one that matches your neighborhood.

Step 1: Find out what your home is worth

Every good listing plan starts with an honest number. Request a free home valuation for Palm Beach County homeowners. We review it against closed sales near you, not just online estimates, and explain how upgrades like a newer roof or impact windows change the result.

Step 2: Read the guide for your city

Central county. Homeowners in the western communities can start with our Lake Worth selling guide, while those closer to the coast should read the Lake Worth Beach guide. Just west of there, the Greenacres guide covers one of the county's busiest family markets.

Western communities. Equestrian, golf and family homes each sell differently in Wellington, which is why we wrote a dedicated Wellington home selling guide.

South county. Boynton Beach has both older coastal homes and large 55+ communities, so its Boynton Beach seller guide splits the advice by area.

North county. Waterfront and club homes dominate the market in the Jupiter selling guide.

For anywhere else in the county, start with our overview of selling a house in Palm Beach County.

Step 3: Choose who will list it

Ask any agent how they will price, photograph and market the home, how showings are handled and how offers are compared. You can meet our Palm Beach County listing agents and walk through the full home selling process before you decide.

If your next home is also in the county, you can browse current Palm Beach County listings while you prepare to sell.
