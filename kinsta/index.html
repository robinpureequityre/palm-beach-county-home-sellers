Selling and Buying at the Same Time

Moving up or downsizing in Palm Beach County

Many Palm Beach County homeowners are not just selling. They are moving up to a bigger home, downsizing into a 55+ community, or relocating closer to family. Selling and buying at the same time is one of the most stressful parts of a move, but with the right order of steps it becomes manageable.

Option 1: Sell first, then buy

Selling first means you know exactly how much equity you have and you never carry two mortgages. The trade-off is that you may need a short-term rental or a lease-back from your buyer while you shop. In a competitive market, this is the safest route for most sellers.

Option 2: Buy first, then sell

Buying first lets you move once and take your time choosing the next home. It works best if you can qualify for both payments or use a bridge loan or home equity line. The risk is carrying two homes if yours takes longer to sell than expected.

Option 3: Line them up together

With good timing, you can sell your current home with a closing date that matches the purchase of your next one, or ask for a few weeks of occupancy after closing. This takes coordination between both transactions, which is where an agent who handles both sides of your move helps.
