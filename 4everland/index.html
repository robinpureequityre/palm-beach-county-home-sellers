Selling a House During a Divorce in Palm Beach County

How divorcing couples in Palm Beach County can price, list and sell the family home with less conflict.

Selling a house during a divorce is stressful, and the home is often the largest shared asset. In Palm Beach County, a clear plan for pricing, preparing and timing the sale can lower tension and protect the value of the home for both spouses.

Decide whether to sell, buy out or wait

Couples usually choose one of three paths: sell now and split the proceeds, have one spouse buy out the other, or keep the home for a set period, often until children finish school. Your attorney and the settlement agreement will decide which option applies.

Agree on a value first

Disagreement over price is one of the most common sticking points. Start with an independent number by requesting a free home value estimate, and consider a formal appraisal if a buyout is on the table.

Choose one agent both spouses trust

A neutral listing agent keeps communication even, sends updates to both parties and presents every offer to both at the same time. Learn more about selling a home during a divorce in Florida.

Keep the process simple

  • Decide in writing who pays the mortgage, taxes and repairs until closing
  • Agree on a minimum acceptable price in advance
  • Set showing rules if one spouse still lives in the home
  • Have both parties sign the listing agreement and the contract
